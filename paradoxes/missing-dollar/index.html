01 The paradox

Three friends share a hotel room that costs $30, paying $10 each. The manager then realises the room is only $25 and gives the bellhop $5 to return. The bellhop keeps $2 as a tip and gives each friend $1 back. So each friend paid $9. Three times $9 is $27, and the bellhop has $2: that makes $29. Where did the other dollar go?

02 Make a guess

Where is the missing dollar?

Nothing is missing. The $2 the bellhop kept is already part of the $27 the friends paid, so adding it again counts it twice. Follow every coin below.

    04 Why it works

    • 🪙

      Follow the money

      At the end, the hotel has $25, the bellhop has $2 and the friends have $3 back. That’s all $30, every coin accounted for.

    • 🔁

      The $2 got counted twice

      The $27 the friends paid already includes the bellhop’s $2. Adding the $2 on top counts it a second time.

    • 🎩

      A sleight of hand with words

      The riddle sounds like careful bookkeeping, but it compares $29 with $30 for no reason. Nothing says those two numbers should match.
