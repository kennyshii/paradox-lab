01 The paradox

Imagine a hotel with infinitely many rooms, numbered 1, 2, 3 and on forever. Tonight every single room is taken. A traveller walks in and asks for a room. Any normal hotel would say “Sorry, we’re full.” But this isn’t a normal hotel. Can the manager find the traveller a bed without throwing anyone out?

02 Make a guess

The hotel is completely full. Can it take more guests?

The full hotel can always make room: for one guest, for five, even for a bus with infinitely many passengers. Nobody has to leave; everybody just moves. Try it below.

04 Why it works

  • 🚪

    Nobody is ever homeless

    Every guest is told exactly which room to move to, and there is always a next room. No one falls off the end, because there is no end.

  • ♾️

    “Full” isn’t “no room”

    In a finite hotel, full means every room is used up. In an infinite one, you can be full and still make space by shuffling everyone along.

  • 🧮

    Infinity isn’t a big number

    Add one, add five, even double it: the hotel is still exactly as full. Infinity behaves like a different kind of thing, not just a very large count.
