A field guide

Not all paradoxes are created equal.

The philosopher W. V. O. Quine sorted them into three families. Every lab is tagged with one.

  • Veridical Absurd, but true.

    The conclusion sounds impossible, yet the argument is sound. Your intuition is what needs fixing.
  • Falsidical A clever trick.

    A convincing argument reaches a false conclusion. The fun is finding the broken step.
  • Antinomy Logic, broken.

    Accepted rules of reasoning collide. Resolving it means changing the rules themselves.
