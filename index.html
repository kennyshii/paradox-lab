Interactive paradoxes

Arguments that feel wrong, and why.

Each paradox here is a small lab: make a guess, play with the idea, then see the math or logic that explains what your intuition missed. Some are true, some are tricks, and some break logic itself.

  • Veridical

    Counterintuitive, but true. The conclusion sounds absurd, yet the argument is sound.

  • Falsidical

    Seems true, but has a hidden flaw. A convincing argument reaches a false conclusion.

  • Antinomy

    A genuine contradiction. Accepted rules of reasoning collide with each other.
